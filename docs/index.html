Ghostwriter

The ghost that keeps your stories.

Tell it a memory out loud: your grandmother's kitchen, a strange night in the hostel, the letter nobody in the family can explain. A ghost living in your laptop writes it down, keeps it, and haunts you with it later.

“That memory was never given to me.”

What the ghost does

It listens

Record in the browser or drop in a voice note. Whisper writes down every word, in Hindi, in English, or in the mix most of us actually speak.

It remembers

Gemma 4 gives each story a title and a short summary, and notes who was there, where and when, and the objects, recipes and sayings worth keeping.

It notices what you left out

After every story the ghost raises its unfinished business. Answer out loud, and your answer is kept beside the story it completes.

“You never told me where the brass pot went...”

It holds a séance

Ask the archive anything. The ghost answers only from what you told it, cites the story it drew on, and admits what it was never told rather than make something up.

It lights a candle for each memory

Your stories stand as candles on a wall strung with marigolds. The ones you haven't visited in a while burn low. Open one, and it flares back to life.

It speaks, and it retells

The Archivist can read its answers aloud in a low, echoing voice, and retell any memory as a campfire bhoot-kahani: always labelled as a retelling, never replacing what you said.

Nothing leaves the laptop

Your voice stays home

Recordings, transcripts and stories live in one folder on your disk. There is no cloud, no account and no analytics.

The lights can go out

Every model runs locally, so it works with the internet switched off. The page doesn't even load fonts from the web.

No bill, no expiry

No API keys and no subscription. Export the whole archive as one file that any browser can open, years from now.

Swap the brains

Model names are one line in a settings file. Use a smaller Gemma on an older laptop, or a larger Whisper for better Hindi.

How it works

  1. Your voicebrowser recording
  2. →
  3. ffmpeg16 kHz audio
  4. →
  5. Whispertranscript
  6. →
  7. Gemma 4validated JSON
  8. →
  9. SQLite FTS5searchable archive
  10. →
  11. Séancegrounded answers
  12. →
  13. Piperthe ghost's voice

Gemma runs through Ollama and streams its words to the page as they are written, so the answer appears like ink from an invisible hand. Search is SQLite's built-in full-text index, which finds Hindi and English alike. When nothing in your stories matches a question, Gemma isn't asked at all.

Real numbers, ordinary laptop

Measured on an Intel i7-13620H with 16 GB of RAM and no graphics card doing the work:

Writing down a recording (Whisper small)about 35 seconds per minute of audio
Understanding a story (Gemma 4 e4b)about 30 seconds
Gemma's writing speed11 to 14 tokens (pieces of words) a second
A séance answerfirst words in 7 to 19 seconds
The ghost's voice11 seconds of speech made in 1.2 seconds

Run it yourself

Windows, Python 3.11, ffmpeg and Ollama. The full steps are in the README.

ollama pull gemma4:e4b
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts\setup.ps1
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts\demo.ps1

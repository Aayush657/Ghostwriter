Ghostwriter

The ghost that keeps your stories.

How Ghostwriter works

  1. 1 Tell it a story. Speak into the microphone or upload a recording.
  2. 2 The ghost writes it down. It gives it a title and notes who, where and when.
  3. 3 Ask the Séance. Ask anything about your stories; it answers only from them.

Candles burn lower when you haven't opened a memory for a while. Open it to relight it.

Ghostwriter

The ghost that keeps your stories.

Keep the stories your family tells (the campfire legend, the night the lights went out, the scarecrow that walked) before they're forgotten. Everything stays on this computer.

Ghostwriter is home to the Archivist, a kind ghost who writes your stories down and remembers every word.

 

How Ghostwriter works

  1. 1 Tell a story. Speak into the microphone, upload a recording, or write it down.
  2. 2 The Archivist writes it down. It gives the story a title and notes who, where and when, then asks what it still wonders about.
  3. 3 Ask the Séance. Ask anything about your stories; it answers only from them and shows which ones it used.

The candle wall

Every story you give the Archivist becomes a candle. Open one to read it.

    Séance

    Ask a question about your stories. The Archivist answers only from them, and numbers the stories it used, like this [1].

    Give the Archivist a story

    Tell it out loud, in English, or upload a recording you already have. Short stories take about a minute to write down.

    Your browser will ask to use the microphone. The recording stays on this computer.

    Write a story down

    No microphone, a noisy room, or a story that needs a second try? Write it here, the way you would tell it. The Archivist reads it just like a recording.

    Misspelled words are underlined: right-click one for suggestions. Common typos are fixed as you type; press Backspace straight after a fix to undo it. Your draft is kept on this computer if you leave the page.

    About Ghostwriter

    Ghostwriter keeps the stories your family tells. The Archivist is the ghost who lives in it: it listens, writes each story down, and answers questions about them.

    What everything does

    Private by design
